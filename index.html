Background

About

I am a PhD candidate at The Ohio State University and the NSF AI-EDGE Institute, advised by Prof. Jia (Kevin) Liu. I work on federated learning, continual learning, and multi-objective optimization. My research connects theoretical guarantees with practical systems: how can we learn efficiently across resource-constrained devices, and retain knowledge as tasks change?

In federated learning, I developed FSL-SAGE (ICML 2025), combining convergence analysis with implementations on geo-distributed GPU systems. My work on PMF-CL studies memory-efficient continual learning through Pareto-optimal solutions for conflicting tasks. I also study the theoretical foundations of in-context learning in transformers.

During my 2026 research internship at Emergence AI, I worked on verifiable code generation, developing Lean 4 evaluation metrics to assess whether generated formal specifications capture the intended behavior. This work led to Beyond Type-checking, accepted at the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on AI for Verifiable Coding, and an accompanying technical blog post.

Previously, I worked with Prof. Philip Schniter at OSU on image inverse problems, robust denoising, learned regularization, and approximate message passing. Before joining OSU, I worked at the Indian Institute of Science with Prof. Yogesh Simmhan, Dr. Malati Hegde, and Mr. SVR Anand, developing and deploying low-cost wireless sensor networks for air-quality monitoring.

Outside research, I enjoy racquet sports, hiking, singing, playing guitar, and reading.
